Privacy policy

This policy covers Monetka for Android (com.coincasino.app), published by Intelligence Labs. Contact: toy.store.od@gmail.com.

Your game stays on your device

Monetka has no registration, developer-operated server or cloud account. Your selected coin, settings, toss totals and recent results are stored locally. We do not upload your toss history.

Advertising and consent

The free version uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. The SDK may process IP-derived approximate location, app/ad interactions, technical diagnostics and device/account identifiers for advertising, measurement and fraud prevention. Transfers use TLS. Available choices depend on your region and consent.

Where required, Monetka offers a privacy-options button in About to revisit your choices. Android also provides advertising-ID controls. PRO disables new advertising requests. Google handles previously collected information under its own policies.

See Google Privacy Policy and Google advertising information.

Purchasing PRO

Google Play handles payment. Monetka does not receive your payment-card details. A Google-signed purchase receipt is stored on your device to verify PRO and keep it available offline; purchase ownership is rechecked with Google Play when available. See Google's privacy policy for payment-service data handling.

Retention, deletion and your rights

Local game and purchase evidence remain until you clear app storage or uninstall. Clearing local evidence does not cancel a purchase; it can be restored through Google Play. We have no developer database of gameplay or accounts to delete. Contact us for privacy questions or requests; contact Google for information held by its services and applicable retention periods. Depending on your location, you may have rights of access, correction, deletion, restriction or objection and a right to contact a supervisory authority.

Adult audience

Monetka is intended for adults aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

This website and support

This static website uses no analytics scripts, advertising or cookies set by us. It is hosted by GitHub Pages, whose infrastructure may process connection information under the GitHub Privacy Statement. If you email support, we use your message and contact address to respond and resolve the request.

Changes

We will publish updates on this page with a revised date.
